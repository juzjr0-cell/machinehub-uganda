# Stage 9 — Wallet, Transactions & Withdrawal Review

This stage completes the user-side money movement UX in SANDBOX mode.

## Added
- Unified transaction feed
- Contribution, activation-fee and distribution records
- Wallet balance based on posted business revenue distributions minus non-rejected withdrawals
- Withdrawal request with Uganda mobile-money number validation
- Minimum withdrawal of UGX 5,000
- Admin withdrawal review endpoint
- Clear pending-review status

## Important
No real mobile-money transfer is performed. Production withdrawals require a compliant merchant/payment-provider setup, secure credentials, KYC/AML controls, reconciliation and any required regulatory approvals.

Business revenue distributions should only be posted after actual business revenue/expense records and the applicable contractual terms have been verified.

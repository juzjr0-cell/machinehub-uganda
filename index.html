OUR APPROACH

Built around real business activity

MachineHub Uganda is designed around identifiable business units rather than artificial balances or guaranteed returns.

Business results can change. Any revenue sharing or distribution must depend on actual business performance and applicable contractual terms.

✓ Business-unit records
✓ Revenue and expense tracking
✓ Transaction history
✓ Clear risk disclosures

Important information

This public website is a demonstration. Payment processing is not enabled here and no profit is guaranteed.

The UGX 10,000 activation amount, where used, is an account activation/service fee and is separate from any business-unit contribution.

Before accepting public funds or offering any regulated financial product, the operating structure and required approvals must be confirmed with the relevant Ugandan authorities and licensed payment partners.

How MachineHub works

Our goal is to make every business unit understandable before you decide to participate.

1

Create an account

Register with your Uganda mobile number and complete required verification.

2

Review a business

Read the business model, minimum contribution, period, costs and risks.

3

The current public website is a demonstration and does not process real money.

4

Track the results

View contributions, business records and any distributions that are actually supported by the business results and terms.

OUR APPROACH

Built around real business activity

MachineHub Uganda is designed around identifiable business units rather than artificial balances or guaranteed returns.

Business performance can change. Any revenue sharing or distribution must be based on actual business performance and applicable contractual terms.

✓ Business-unit records
✓ Revenue and expense tracking
✓ Transaction history
✓ Clear risk disclosures

Important information

This public website is a demonstration. Payment processing is not enabled here and no profit is guaranteed.

If an account activation/service fee is introduced, it should be presented separately from business-unit contributions.

Before accepting public funds or offering any regulated financial product, the operating structure and required approvals should be confirmed with the relevant Ugandan authorities and licensed payment partners.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this website already processing real money?

No. This public GitHub Pages website is a demonstration frontend. Real payments must use an approved production payment arrangement.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Business performance can vary. The platform should never describe business participation as a guaranteed-profit product.

What is the UGX 10,000 activation fee?

If used, it is an account activation/service fee. It should be clearly separated from any business-unit contribution.

Can I withdraw money?

Withdrawal functionality belongs in the secured application/API. The public website does not process withdrawals.
